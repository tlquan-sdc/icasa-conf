Call for Papers

Research scope for responsible applications of AI in smart and sustainable agriculture across ASEAN.

Conference scope

Invitation to contribute

The full-paper submission deadline is 1 January 2027. Review policies and proceedings information are maintained on this website.

Conference tracks

ICASA welcomes original research and applied work across the following interdisciplinary areas:

  1. AI-based Smart Agriculture and Machine Learning
  2. Computer Vision, Remote Sensing and Crop Phenotyping
  3. Agricultural Biology and Computational Bioinformatics
  4. Plant Genomics, Smart Breeding and Biotechnology
  5. Agricultural Microbiology, Microbiomes and Biological Control
  6. Plant Health, Pathology and Precision Crop Protection
  7. IoT, Smart Sensors and Edge AI for Agriculture
  8. Agricultural Robotics, UAVs and Autonomous Systems
  9. Soil, Water and Precision Nutrient Management
  10. Climate-smart Agriculture and Environmental Sustainability
  11. Smart Aquaculture, Livestock and Animal Health
  12. Agricultural Data, Digital Twins and Decision Support
  13. Food Quality, Post-harvest Technology and Supply Chains
  14. ASEAN Agritech, Policy and Rural Innovation

Requirements

Manuscripts must follow the official conference instructions. Authors should check the formatting requirements carefully before submitting a paper.

  • The registration fee covers papers of up to 10 pages. Each page beyond 10 pages is subject to an additional fee of $15 (385,000 VND) per page.
  • Papers must be written in English.
  • Submissions must be original, not previously published and not under review elsewhere.
  • The manuscript must fit at least one official ICASA 2027 conference track.
  • Use the official conference manuscript template when it becomes available and submit the review manuscript as a PDF with all fonts embedded.
  • Enter the title, abstract, author names, affiliations, email addresses and topics accurately in Microsoft CMT.
The review model, PDF file-size limit and supplementary-material policy will be published before the submission portal opens.

Submission through Microsoft CMT

Authors create or use a Microsoft CMT account, enter the paper metadata and author list, select suitable topics, upload the manuscript and retain the paper ID for later revisions.
