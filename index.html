Purpose

From research question
to field application.

ICASA brings together computer science, agricultural engineering, environmental research and regional development communities.

This edition focuses on the conditions that make technology useful in practice: local data, responsible deployment, resilient farming systems and collaboration across ASEAN.

Conference scope

Research themes

Featured areas from a broader interdisciplinary programme spanning AI, agricultural engineering, life sciences and regional innovation.

  1. 01

    AI-based Smart Agriculture and Machine Learning

    AI models, predictive analytics, farm intelligence and responsible decision-making.

  2. 02

    Computer Vision, Remote Sensing and Crop Phenotyping

    Imaging, satellite and UAV data, crop monitoring, yield estimation and disease detection.

  3. 03

    Agricultural Biology and Computational Bioinformatics

    Biological data analysis, systems biology and computational methods for agricultural research.

  4. 04

    Plant Genomics, Smart Breeding and Biotechnology

    Genomics, marker-assisted breeding, gene–environment interactions and crop improvement.

  5. 05

    IoT, Smart Sensors and Edge AI for Agriculture

    Connected sensing, embedded intelligence, field monitoring and real-time control.

  6. 06

    Soil, Water and Precision Nutrient Management

    Soil health, irrigation, fertility assessment and site-specific input optimisation.

  7. 07

    Climate-smart Agriculture and Environmental Sustainability

    Climate resilience, resource efficiency, biodiversity and circular agricultural systems.

  8. 08

    Smart Aquaculture, Livestock and Animal Health

    Monitoring, welfare, feeding, disease prevention and intelligent production systems.

  9. 09

    ASEAN Agritech, Policy and Rural Innovation

    Technology adoption, governance, entrepreneurship and inclusive regional development.
