02
Paper submission · Microsoft CMT

Paper Submission

The full-paper submission deadline is 1 January 2027. Prepare and verify every file before uploading it to the official conference portal.

Requirements

Manuscripts must follow the Springer Nature proceedings instructions. Authors should check the formatting requirements carefully before submitting a paper.

  • Full papers must contain at least 6 pages and must not exceed 10 pages. Additional-page charges apply beyond 6 pages.
  • Papers must be written in English.
  • Submissions must be original, not previously published and not under review elsewhere.
  • The manuscript must fit at least one official ICASA 2027 conference track.
  • Use the official Springer Nature proceedings template and submit the review manuscript as a PDF with all fonts embedded.
  • Enter the title, abstract, author names, affiliations, email addresses and topics accurately in Microsoft CMT.
The review model, PDF file-size limit and supplementary-material policy will be published before the submission portal opens.

Submission procedure

All full papers must be submitted through the official ICASA 2027 Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT) portal. Do not submit through a CMT link belonging to another conference.

Official submission portal

Microsoft CMT · Available soon

Submission process

  1. 01
    Create or access your account

    Create a Microsoft CMT account or sign in with the email address you will use for conference correspondence.

  2. 02
    Open the ICASA 2027 portal

    Use only the conference-specific link published on this website and review the current submission instructions.

  3. 03
    Prepare the manuscript

    Use the Springer Nature template, convert the paper to PDF and ensure that all fonts are embedded.

  4. 04
    Create a new submission

    Open the Author Console, enter the title, abstract, authors, affiliations and relevant conference topics, then upload the PDF manuscript.

  5. 05
    Add supporting files if requested

    Upload supplementary material only when permitted by the final submission policy.

  6. 06
    Review and confirm

    Check all metadata and files before finalising the submission. Retain the paper ID and confirmation email.

Revisions

Submit a revised manuscript through the same paper record when requested and before the stated deadline.

Communication

Check your registered email address and the CMT Author Console regularly for decisions and reviewer comments.

Final submission

Accepted papers must satisfy the camera-ready, publishing-agreement, registration and presentation requirements.
